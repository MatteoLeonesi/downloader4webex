Video Downloader for Webex

How to download a recording in 4 steps

1
Open a Webex recording
Navigate to any recorded lesson or meeting on webex.com.
2
Press play
Let the video play for a few seconds. The extension listens for the video stream in the background.
3
Click the extension icon
Open the Video Downloader for Webex popup from the Chrome toolbar. You'll see the detected MP4 file.
4
Download
Hit Download — Chrome will save the MP4 directly to your computer.
Tip: When a video is detected, a badge number appears on the extension icon and a notification pops up — you don't need to keep the popup open while playing.
